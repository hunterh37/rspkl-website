Account Recovery & FAQ

I lost my account — how do I recover it?
Email support@rspkl.com from the address on the account with your log-in name and creation details. Recovery is handled within 48 hours.
My purchase did not arrive.
Deliveries are instant in-game. If anything is missing after a relog, email support@rspkl.com with your order ID — the shop ledger reconciles every 15 minutes.
How do I report a player?
Use ::report in-game with the player name, or open a ticket with video evidence. Cheating is reviewed within 24 hours.
Someone claiming to be staff asked for my password.
It is an impersonator. Staff never ask for passwords or hold items. Screenshot it and send it to support@rspkl.com immediately.
